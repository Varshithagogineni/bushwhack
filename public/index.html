How to play

  • Three modes: ☠ Showdown (last one standing), 💎 Gem Grab (2 teams, hold 10 gems), ⭐ Bounty (knockouts earn stars).
  • Showdown poison closes in from the edges after 30 seconds. Stay inside the circle.
  • Bushes hide you from anyone who isn't right next to you, until you shoot.
  • Break crates for power cubes: +health and +damage. Knocked-out brawlers drop theirs too.
  • Land hits to charge your Super (yellow ring under you), then unleash it.
  • Knocked out? You're not done. Come back as a 👻 ghost, float through walls and drop haunts that slow and hurt the survivors.
  • Ammo (3 bars under you) refills on its own. Health heals when you stop fighting.

Laptop
